# Ragnarok MVP — Prontera walk

1. **Pitch.** A top-down town explorer where you walk a novice adventurer through a slice of Prontera, greet NPCs, and bump into wandering Porings while the real map collision comes from ragassets.
2. **View and dimension.** 2D, top-down, camera follows the hero.
3. **Main action.** Walk and explore.
4. **Controls.**

| Action | Keyboard | Touch | Gamepad |
|--------|----------|-------|---------|
| Move | WASD / arrows | Virtual stick | Left stick / d-pad |
| Talk | E / Enter | USE button | A / bottom face |
| Pause | Esc (shell) | Pause in shell | Start |

5. **Goal and end.** No fail state in MVP: explore freely; talking to NPCs shows a short line. Play again resets position.
6. **What keeps it fresh.** Real map layout from GAT, authentic sprites from the asset CDN, NPC and mob placement.
7. **Difficulty.** Flat — exploration only.
8. **Limits.** Max 12 Porings on screen chunk; hero speed 95; map chunk 96×96 cells; tile 8 px.
9. **Fun test.** We will know whether walking on real Prontera collision with RO sprites feels like Ragnarok.
10. **Screens.** Title, play (no separate end screen in MVP).
